Our story

A recipe rediscovered in Bohol.

Our story starts in 2015, when our mother, Nenecitas, went home to Bohol for a fiesta and tasted an old family ice cream recipe. She came back to Alae, Manolo Fortich unable to forget it — and called on her Tito to teach the family how it was done the traditional way, by hand.

He passed away not long after, but the sorbetes he taught us to make became ours to carry forward. We still churn it his way: coconut milk, or “gata,” in place of dairy, fresh local fruit, and ice and salt instead of freezers — the old method that gives sorbetes its true texture and taste.

What began as small batches sold to neighbors in Alae is now Nenecitas Sorbetes — still family-run, still made the same way, named after the woman who brought the recipe home.
